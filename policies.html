📌 Store Policies

Super Ninja Monkey Cards is built on transparency, protection, and good vibes. Please read below for shipping, condition, and buying information.

📦 Shipping & Packaging

All cards are packaged securely with collector-level care.

  • Cards are shipped in penny sleeves + top loaders or card savers
  • Bubble mailer or secure box for higher-value items
  • Tracking is included on most shipments
  • Orders typically ship within 1–3 business days

🧐 Card Condition

Card condition is always shown as clearly as possible through photos and descriptions.

  • Raw cards are not guaranteed to grade Gem Mint
  • Please review photos carefully before purchasing
  • If you have questions, feel free to reach out before buying

🔁 Returns

Returns are accepted if an item arrives damaged or is not as described. Buyer satisfaction matters, and we aim to resolve issues quickly and fairly.

For purchases through eBay, all transactions follow eBay’s buyer protection policies.

🤝 Consignment Items

Some inventory may be sold on behalf of a trusted collector partner through consignment. All consignment items are handled with the same care and honesty as our personal listings.

💬 Questions?

If you ever have questions about a card, shipping, bundles, or pricing, please reach out through our Contact page.
